# Research Themes

These are organized by scientific question, not by paper. Each page follows the same structure: the question, a brief note on where the field currently stands, my approach, and what remains open.

| Theme | Question |
|---|---|
| [EO for Socioeconomic Systems](eo-for-socioeconomic-systems.md) | Can Earth observation explain socioeconomic outcomes independently of survey data? |
| [Spatial Intelligence](spatial-intelligence.md) | How should heterogeneous spatial units at different administrative levels be represented jointly? |
| [AI for Official Statistics](ai-for-official-statistics.md) | How can administratively-collected data be integrated with sensor data without collapsing its structure? |
| [Disaster Resilience and Recovery](disaster-resilience-and-recovery.md) | Can a general spatial representation capture propagated impact, not only direct damage? |

The open questions are stated as open. Where I have a working position I say so, and where I do not I would rather say that than manufacture one.
