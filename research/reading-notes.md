### Foundation Models for Remote Sensing and Earth Observation: A Survey

*Read {{DATE}}*

Useful mainly as a map of what pre-training objectives have actually been tried across modalities, and of how consistently the field defines multimodality as combinations of sensors. Optical with SAR, spectral with spatial, imagery with elevation. Every one of these shares a pixel geometry.

The gap I keep coming back to is that a modality with no pixel geometry, such as administratively-collected statistics, does not fit the existing taxonomy at all. It is not clear to me whether that is because the problem is not interesting, or because it has not been framed as a representation problem yet. I think it is the second.
