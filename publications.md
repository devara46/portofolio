{{AUTHORS}} ({{YEAR}}). *{{FULL TITLE}}*. International Journal of Remote Sensing and Earth Sciences. {{VOLUME, PAGES, DOI}}

### 2. Remote sensing and geospatial data for accessibility and sustainable development in Indonesia

{{AUTHORS}} ({{YEAR}}). *{{FULL TITLE}}*. {{PROCEEDINGS NAME}}. {{PAGES, LINK}}

Conceptual paper, not an empirical study. The turning point in framing: the question shifted from what land looks like to how spatial arrangement affects people's access to services and opportunity. This is where relational thinking about space entered the work.

### 3. Granular school distance mapping using Dijkstra's algorithm, West Nusa Tenggara

{{AUTHORS}} ({{YEAR}}). *{{FULL TITLE}}*. Seminar Nasional Official Statistics {{YEAR}}. {{PAGES, LINK}}

First implementation of network-based spatial reasoning. Distance measured along an actual road network rather than as straight-line proximity, at a granularity useful for administrative decisions. The transportation edge family in the current graph framework descends directly from this.

### 4. Machine learning and transfer learning for standardized job classification, West Nusa Tenggara

{{AUTHORS}} ({{YEAR}}). *{{FULL TITLE}}*. Seminar Nasional Official Statistics {{YEAR}}. {{PAGES, LINK}}

Conducted concurrently with the school distance work. Applying machine learning inside official statistics production rather than to imagery. This is the origin of my concern with the structure of statistical data: hierarchical classification systems, semantically defined categories, and error propagation across levels. It is the empirical basis for the position in [AI for Official Statistics](research/themes/ai-for-official-statistics.md).

### 5. Intermodal logistics and regional competitiveness, Central Java

{{AUTHORS}} ({{YEAR}}). *{{FULL TITLE}}*. Jurnal Dinamika Ekonomi Pembangunan. {{VOLUME, PAGES, DOI}}

Extended relational reasoning from access to economic connectivity. Treating connectivity as a property of a region rather than of a route is what makes typed lateral edges in the graph framework a substantive design choice rather than a technical one.

### 6. High Yield, Limited Prosperity: Diagnosing the Food System Gap Between Agricultural Productivity and Village Economic Activation in East Java

{{AUTHORS}}. Under review.

Convergence point. NDVI and night-time lights crossed into a village-level welfare typology, with official statistics used for validation rather than supervision. Established the productivity to prosperity conversion gap as a real and spatially structured phenomenon. No public preprint; available on request, see [About](about.md). Discussed in detail under [Current Research](research/current-research.md).

---

### 7. Generalizability extension, Southeast Sulawesi

{{AUTHORS}}. Submitted.

Tests whether the East Java framing survives a change of regional structure: three commodities instead of one, and an economy split between maritime and mining sub-economies where night-time lights no longer indicate agriculture-derived prosperity. The limits exposed by this study are the direct motivation for moving from fixed indicators to learned representations.
